# VeriMark design brief — INIT-C

Status: original exploration, approval pending. Working name only; no trademark clearance implied.

Confirmed input: `setup-inputs/PROJECT_DETAILS.md` describes small organizations issuing accountable credentials and account-free public checks. The dispatch authorizes local documentation, commits and isolated prototypes despite the setup template's stop instruction. The canonical PRD and stack were templates when inspected; product and architecture owners reconcile those independently. No production stack decision is made here.

Goal: make a credential's issuer, recipient, achievement and lifecycle status understandable before asking someone to trust it. Primary users are student-organization officers, training administrators, recipients and third-party verifiers. Avoid prestige seals, fake university endorsements, invented adoption metrics and claims of accreditation.

Acceptance: three original directions, six working screen views each; desktop and narrow mobile layouts; local review/approve issuance; account-free recipient access; keyboard controls and explicit exceptional states. Files are limited to docs/design, docs/frontend, prototypes/design and the INIT-C handoff. Verify with `node prototypes/design/build.cjs`, `node --check prototypes/design/build.cjs` and `node prototypes/design/check.cjs` using the installed browser. No package install, backend, production app, cloud or deployment.

Trust copy must say: QR opens a link, not proof; compare issuer, recipient, achievement and date; reviewed issuer identity is not accreditation; a service outage is unable to verify, never invalid. MVP verification is an online authoritative-record check in the proposed product. Offline prototypes simulate that check and confer no validity. No recipient wallet, cryptocurrency or portable signing is included.

Open human decisions: choose a direction, approve public-field privacy policy, confirm issuer-review wording, test comprehension with prospective users, and validate working name. Recommendation is Civic Ledger, pending human selection. No user research has been conducted.
